How search and source notes work

Recommendations do not confirm that a book is currently on its shelf.

Search uses the pretrained all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence model, combined with title and word matching. It compares your request with the catalog. It does not invent answers or book contents. Only source-backed descriptions are included in semantic search; records still under research are searchable by their listed title and author. An empty summary means the description has not yet passed its source check.

All queries stay on the computer running this kiosk. The first start downloads the model; later searches use the local copy. Scores are ranking signals, not probabilities. The model is strongest in English.
